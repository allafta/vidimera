# Deferral register

The living register of consciously deferred work, per the process discipline in
`docs/concept/SCOPING-GUIDE.md` section 7: every deferral carries the trigger that
un-defers it. An entry leaves this table only when the behavior lands or the
deferral is explicitly retired. Numbers are stable identifiers, not positions: a
gap is an entry that has left, and a new entry takes the next number never yet used.

Measured, not inferred: six consecutive refusing `finalize()` calls against a sealed signing record carrying one broken signature took the sidecar from 5 entries to 11 under a real clock — one fresh orphan per call, the readings after each being 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 — while the chain stayed at 5 throughout; under a fixed instant the same six calls left it at 6, every call after the first re-signing byte-identical bytes onto one key.
